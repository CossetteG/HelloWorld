Stepping Stone Temporary Living

Welcome to Stepping Stone Apartments!

We offer non-luxury housing for folks down on their luck, to help you get a roof over your head and a mailing address. Rent a room for one night or for a month.

Floorplan

Rules for Tenants

  1. Rooms are $10 per night or $100 per month
  2. Rooms are restricted to 1 room per family
  3. You must make minimum wage or less to rent a room
  4. You must be 14 years old or older to rent a room
  5. Pay rent in advance or you will lose your room
  6. You must have valid id in order to get a room
  7. Check in starts at 2pm
  8. Check out ends at 11am
  9. Mailboxes sold separately- $15 per month
  10. No cats, dogs, or other large pets
  11. Do not. lose your key. $25 penalty
  12. No violence inside the building
  13. No harrassment of employees or fellow tenants
  14. We reserve the right to ban anyone from the premises
  15. We will call the cops to escort people out if we need to

Policy and Procedure for Employees

  • There are 3 shifts: Morning 6am - 2pm, Afternoon 12pm - 8pm, Night 7pm - 2am
  • Morning shift handles check out
  • Afternoon shift handles most of check in
  • During 12pm-2pm, both morning and afternoon shifts check all the necessary rooms to verify that:
    1. The rooms that have not been paid for have been vacated
    2. Personal belongings have not been left behind
    3. The room is safe, livable, and mostly clean
  • The night shift handles late check ins and process mail
  • You must be 21 years old or older to be employed here
  • Do not hesitate to call the cops if a tenant is endangering himself or others

Utah is currently in a housing emergency. Currently in Salt Lake City, there are over 2,000 people living unhoused. Our goal here at Stepping Stone is to provide housing to the lowest income bracket- which mostly includes people with no income. Having a roof over your head and a mailing address to put on job applications/bank accounts/etc can be the difference between being outcast from society and being normal. Our housing is not designed to be permanent- it is designed to be a stepping stone between being unhoused and living a more comfortable life. Our rooms are government-subsidized and OSCHA compliant, so that you can have a safe place to sleep at night.

Our secondary goal is to shift the housing market downward- proving that low-income housing is profitable, and therefore making housing more affordable for everyone. (Disclaimer- this entire operation is hypothetical at the time of this website being made 2025)

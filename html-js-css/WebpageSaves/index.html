Interests and Hobbies

I like to play Blades in the Dark, a table-top role-playing game about a group of scoundrels in the mysterious, crowded city of Doskvol. I play as the game master to create scenarios in which my players can pull a heist and steal some treasure. Me and my boyfriend and 4 of our other good friends get together every Sunday to play.

I also enjoy playing Persona 5, which is a game pretty similar to Blades in style, but different in mechanics. It is a Japanese role-playing game, so I play as the leader of the Phantom Theives, and basically we go into the Metaverse, which is like a parallel universe, and use magic to make bad people repent, basically. I literally decided to play this game just because of how cool I thought the character looked in Super Smash Brothers, and I was right, I really like it.

Aside from games, I enjoy reading quite a bit. I like to read long book series with complicated plots and slow burn romance. I used to be much better at reading books quickly, but I have not been able to read as much since starting college. My current favorite books are listed above, but some honorable mentions include Percy Jackson books, the Sisters Grimm, Tiger's Curse, Daughter of Smoke and Bone, and Spy Family. One of my favorite parts about reading is sometimes, when the book is popular or has good romance, you can join the fandom and read fanfiction for the story.
